# Design Direction

## Product

Personal memory microsite for Satrio and Casviana. The page should feel intimate, calm, and easy to read on a phone before it feels decorative.

## Audience

The primary reader is Casviana, reading the page privately on a mobile device. The language should feel direct, sincere, and personal.

## Identity

- Cosmic editorial mood with a restrained dark canvas
- Cyan used for interaction, navigation, and meaningful highlights
- Existing Space Grotesk retained for a familiar, modern voice
- Personal photos carry the emotional weight; decoration stays secondary

## Layout rules

- One clear action in the hero: open the story and start the music
- Content width stays readable, with generous vertical rhythm between chapters
- Cards group related memories but do not compete with the main story
- Mobile layout is the baseline, not a compressed desktop layout

## Type and colour

- Primary text is white or pale blue on near-black surfaces
- Supporting copy uses the brighter muted token so it remains legible in low brightness
- Title, section heading, body copy, and labels use a clear size ladder
- Glows are reserved for the title and active controls

## Motion

Energy: 2 out of 5. Motion supports orientation and discovery, never reading speed.

Rhythm: 3 out of 5. Scroll reveals are short and staggered without making the page feel delayed.

Accessibility: respect reduced-motion preferences and keep all interactive targets at least 44px tall.

## Voice

Indonesian is the default language. English remains only in the page title, where it works as the personal name of the project. Copy should use consistent capitalization, concrete wording, and first-person warmth without exaggerated promises.
